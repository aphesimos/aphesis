## hypothesis

the fog turns each of its three layers by 137.5°, the golden angle, so their patterns never line up. a sunflower
uses the same angle to place its seeds. if each new seed turns by that angle from the last, the seeds should be
spaced more evenly than with any nearby angle.

## method

300 seeds are placed one after another: seed *i* at *i* turns of the angle and at a distance √*i* from the centre,
so every seed gets the same share of the disc. for each seed the bench finds the distance to its nearest neighbour.
the unevenness is the spread of those 300 distances: their standard deviation over their mean. it is 0 when every
seed stands as far from its closest neighbour as every other.

the bench sweeps the angle from 130° to 145° in steps of 0.02°, drawing the curve as it measures it, then looks
again around the lowest point in steps of 0.0005°. the slider places the seeds for any angle; a click on the curve
moves the slider there.
