The scrollbar of the site: a red disturbance in the stipple along the right edge of whatever scrolls. The browser's own bars are hidden everywhere (`bundle.css`); scrolling itself stays native.

- **At rest** there is nothing. **While an element scrolls**, or while a fine pointer is within 24px of its right edge, accent cells gather there in a soft vertical patch where the thumb would be: its length is the visible share of the content (at least 36px), its place the scroll offset, 6px in from the ends of the element.
- **The look.** 2px cells on the fog's own grid (one canvas pixel per cell, `cellPx(2)`), in a band 12px wide at the edge: densest at the edge and over the thumb's span, thinning over a 26px feather at both ends and away from the edge, with a slow grain along its length. Density stays under .75, so it never reads as a shape. It trembles at 8 fps (the cells reseed every 125ms), grows denser while the element moves, appears over 320ms and fades over 1s with minimum jerk, a second after the scrolling stops or the pointer leaves the edge.
- **Interaction.** With a fine pointer, the 14px along the edge are the scrollbar's: drag the patch to scroll, press above or below it to page (seven eighths of the view, smoothly). The press selects nothing and clicks nothing beneath it. On touch it shows only while scrolling. Keyboard, find in page and screen readers are the browser's and unaffected.
- **Where.** One canvas (`.aph-scroll`, fixed, `z-scroll`: over the content, under the seal and the corners, `pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden`), drawn only while a patch is visible and only when something changed. It finds scrollers by itself: a scroll event anywhere (caught on the document) or the pointer at an edge. Inner scrollers (the archive's and forum's columns, music's panes and queue, the desk and its preview, the book's leaf on a phone) and the document (the laboratory) are handled alike.
- **Colour.** The current `--accent`, read again every 125ms, so it follows the theme and its wave.
- **API.** The runtime starts it at load. `Aphesis.Scroll(el)` returns an element's state; `Aphesis.Scroll.show(el)` lights its patch as if it had scrolled. A page that wants none sets `window.APHESIS_NO_SCROLL = true` before the runtime loads.
- **Don't** bring a native scrollbar back (`scrollbar-width`, `scrollbar-color`, `::-webkit-scrollbar` styling, `scrollbar-gutter`): the bars are hidden with `!important` and nothing is reserved for them, so no layout ever shifts. **Don't** hide overflow that people need to scroll to see: a scroller is found by its `overflow-y: auto | scroll`.
