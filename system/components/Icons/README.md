The icon set: brush strokes with stipple fill, two states each.

- **Provide** a name (`eye`, `sol`, `luna`, `terminator`, `threshold`, `ear`, or a section: `book`, `descent`, `archive`, `laboratory`, `works`, `wall`, `forum`, `music`) and a state (0 or 1). `Aphesis.icon(name, state)` returns inline SVG in `currentColor`; size it with CSS (28 to 64px) and color it `var(--accent)`.
- **Use** the eye for the theme (open = light, closed = dark), the threshold for entering a chamber, the ear for the sound (0 listening, 1 muted; mount the live switch with `Aphesis.Sound.ear`), sol · luna and the terminator for day and night content.
- **Sections.** Eight more marks stand for the sections where their names would crowd (the home's inscription on a phone): `book` (an open codex, lines of text in stipple), `descent` (the threshold's arch with steps going down, the night thickening below), `archive` (a pile of leaves, the top one written on), `laboratory` (a flask, matter settled in its belly, two bubbles rising), `works` (a pair of compasses and the arc they have just drawn), `wall` (courses of stone, a few stones stippled), `forum` (two voices: one speaking in three grains, one answering), `music` (the record ring with stipple grooves, a small centre and the arm). `Aphesis.SECTIONS` lists them in the home's order. State 0 is at rest, 1 is chosen: the same cells gathered toward the mark's heart.
- **Motion.** Put the icon in a control with the class `aph-icon`: hovering or focusing stirs its stipple, two halves against each other, as the ear's arcs do. Add `aph-gather` for the first tap on a section icon (the cells draw in over `duration-slow`); the second tap enters. `icon(name, 1)` is that gathering, still. Draw section icons at 32 to 40px, never below 24.
- Buttons holding an icon need an `aria-label` and a `data-tip`.
- New icons are drawn with `Aphesis.brush` and `Aphesis.stipple` on the same 24-unit grid, then added here.
