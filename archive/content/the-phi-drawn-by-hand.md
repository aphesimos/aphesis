the seal in the corner is a φ, drawn by hand, once. everything else on the site that looks drawn was measured from it.

## one hand

the original is a single filled shape, 385 by 533 units. measured, it gives the brush: a broad nib held at 38°, a stroke about 2.8 times thicker at its fullest than at its thinnest, and ends that taper. the icons are drawn with that brush, and so is the script face of the titles, Aphesis Script.

## the engine

at the center of the site the φ is not shown but drawn again, on every visit. an engine turns a centerline and a width profile into a filled outline: the stem rises from its tapered tip, then the ring is swept around to its free end. the pen speeds up and slows down with minimum-jerk easing, the curve a relaxed hand follows between two points, and it slows in tight curves, as a hand does.

entering a section, the φ closes into Φ and travels to the corner, where it leads back to the center.

## the boil

the ink never sits still. a turbulence filter displaces the edges of the mark, and its seed changes eight times a second, so the outline boils like a drawing made frame by frame. for anyone who asks their system for less motion, the boil stops.
